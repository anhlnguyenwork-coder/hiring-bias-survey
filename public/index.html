DePauw University — Research Study

What this is

You are helping build materials for a research study at DePauw University on how job application writing is evaluated. We are collecting authentic, human-written responses to a common job application question.

What we will do with your writing

Your response will be used as research material. It may be lightly edited, reformatted, or paired with fictional applicant details (a made-up name, school, and work history) before being shown to study participants who will evaluate it. A de-identified copy may be posted in a public research archive when the study is published. Your identity will never be attached to your text.

What we are not doing

This is not a real job application. No employer will see this. There is no hiring decision attached to it.

Time and compensation

About 15–20 minutes. Compensation will be provided via Prolific.

Your rights

Participation is voluntary. You may stop at any point without penalty. Questions: contact the research team via Prolific.

The Question & Rules

Read the question and all rules before writing your response.

The Question

"Explain why you are a good fit for this position based on your past experience."

Answer as if you were applying to the HR Specialist position above, as an early-career candidate — a current senior, recent graduate, or someone in their first year or two of work.

Write about your own real experiences. Jobs, internships, campus roles, volunteer work, group projects, athletics, part-time work — anything where you built a skill this role calls for. Do not invent accomplishments.

Rules — read all of these

Length: 250–350 words. The box will not accept fewer than 225 or more than 400.

Format rules:

  • Continuous prose only — no bullet points, no headings, no numbered lists
  • No greeting and no sign-off — do not write "Dear Hiring Manager" or "Sincerely." Start with your first sentence of substance
  • Be specific — include at least two concrete examples with real detail: what the situation was, what you did, what happened. Where you can, put a number on it
  • Write it once, the way you'd actually send it — we want your natural writing voice

No AI tools — this is required. Your response must be written entirely by you, without AI assistance of any kind. Please type directly into the box — do not paste.
